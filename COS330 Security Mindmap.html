Using the board

Choose a chapter above. Select a branch to unfold its notes, and select a note to read more. Drag a card to rearrange it; drag or swipe the empty desk to pan. Pinch to zoom on touchscreens; use the zoom controls or mouse wheel on a computer. Touch and hold a card, then drag it to reposition it. Hold Shift while scrolling to pan horizontally. The checkboxes mark branches you know. Press / to search and Escape to close panels. Your progress and card positions are saved on this device.
